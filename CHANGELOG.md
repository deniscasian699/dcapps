# Changelog

Notable changes to DC Tools are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/).

---

## [Unreleased]

> Upcoming changes will be listed here before each release.

### Changed in the current Android build

- Interstitial ads have a 120-second minimum interval between displays and
  between new requests; requests occur only near the six-navigation threshold
  after the cooldown, and the count persists across app launches.
- Phones stay in portrait; tablets support portrait and landscape.
- App website, legal links, bug reports and support contact now use RDC Apps domains.

---

## [Latest] — September 2026

### Added

- 200+ tools for images, text, code, design, SEO, security and calculations.
- Search, category filters, favorites and recent tools.
- Free daily usage with a 10-use limit.
- Premium Monthly and Premium Lifetime for unlimited usage, Split Screen,
  appearance and typography customization, and premium app icons.
- Supporter and Supporter Monthly purchases.
- Google AdMob banner, native, interstitial, app-open and rewarded advertising.
- Google consent flow and available advertising privacy options in Settings.
- Optional rewarded ad for one additional tool use when available.

### Changed

- Supporter removes ads while the purchase remains valid.
- Supporter Monthly removes ads and unlocks premium icons while active.
- Premium Monthly and Premium Lifetime do not remove ads.
- Advertising disclosures in the privacy policy and terms.
