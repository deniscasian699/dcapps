## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 🛠️ A Tool Is Not Working

- Update DC Tools and Android System WebView.
- Some tools need an internet connection to obtain additional resources.
- Report the tool name, steps, device model and Android version.
- Do not include private files in public reports.

### ⚡ Daily Usage Limit Reached

The free plan includes 10 tool uses per day. Wait for the next daily reset,
use an optional rewarded ad for +1 use when available, or choose a Premium plan.
Rewarded ads are not always available; a cooldown or unavailable message may appear.

### 💎 Premium Not Activating

1. Use the Google Play account that made the purchase.
2. Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
3. Check your internet connection and reopen the app.
4. If needed, email support with your Google Play Order ID.

### ❤️ Monthly Subscription After Buying Lifetime

Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel a monthly subscription.
Manage subscriptions through your Google Play account:

→ [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads

Choose **Supporter** (one-time) or **Supporter Monthly** (subscription).
The one-time purchase removes ads while valid; the monthly plan removes ads
while active. Ads may return after a refund or subscription expiry.

**Premium Monthly and Premium Lifetime do not remove ads.** They unlock
unlimited usage, Split Screen, appearance and typography settings, and premium icons.

### 🛡️ Advertising Privacy Choices

Available Google advertising privacy options can be reopened from Settings.
Availability depends on the consent requirements for your region and account.
This advertising option is not shown while an ad-removal entitlement is active.

## 💳 Billing & Refunds

Billing and refunds are handled by **Google Play** under its applicable policy.
Use your Google Play order history to request a refund. For help, email support
with your Order ID; never send payment-card details.
